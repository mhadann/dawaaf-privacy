Delete Your Dawaaf Account

At Dawaaf, we respect your privacy and your right to control your personal information.

If you no longer wish to use Dawaaf, you can request the deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time.

This option is available to both Dawaaf customers and Umrah Badal performers.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Contact Dawaaf Support using the Request Account Deletion button below.
  2. Send the pre-filled account deletion request through WhatsApp.
  3. Provide the email address associated with your Dawaaf account so we can identify it. Do not send your password or payment card details.
  4. Our support team will verify your request and assist you with the account deletion process.

Request Account Deletion

You do not need to provide your password to request account deletion.

What Data Will Be Deleted?

After your request has been verified, Dawaaf will process the deletion of your account and associated personal information, except for information that must be retained for legitimate legal or regulatory reasons.

Depending on your account and how you have used Dawaaf, this may include:

  • Account and profile information.
  • Contact details associated with your account.
  • Personal information provided when using Dawaaf.
  • Other account-related data that is not required to be retained.

What Data May Be Retained?

Certain information may need to be retained where required or permitted by applicable law, including:

  • Transaction and payment-related records.
  • Accounting and tax records.
  • Information needed to resolve disputes.
  • Information required for fraud prevention, security, or legal compliance.

Any retained information will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable legal requirements.
