About Dawaaf and these terms

These terms describe the use of Dawaaf by customers and performers. Dawaaf helps customers arrange Cumro (Umrah Badal), a physical service performed by an assigned performer on behalf of a beneficiary. The beneficiary is the person for whom the service is requested.

The purchase is for the physical service. Progress updates and evidence videos document its performance; they are not a separate digital product.

Booking and beneficiary information

The customer chooses a performer and provides the beneficiary’s name, status or eligibility, and relationship to the customer. Check these details and the selected performer before paying. Contact Support promptly if anything is incorrect.

Provide accurate information and make sure you are entitled to provide information about the beneficiary and request the service on their behalf. Provide only the information requested. If you are unsure whether the service is appropriate for the beneficiary, seek suitable religious guidance before ordering.

Price and payments

The current fixed customer price is USD 300 for one Cumro service, regardless of the selected performer. Review the amount and currency shown at checkout before confirming payment.

Stripe processes payment through its payment interface. Dawaaf does not store full payment card numbers or card security codes. Dawaaf uses payment references, amounts, currency and payment status to confirm and manage the order.

An unfinished checkout or a pending payment is not an active assignment. The order becomes a paid assignment after the backend confirms successful payment and checks the selected performer’s eligibility. If payment status is unclear, check your order or contact Support before attempting another payment.

Assignment and the seven-day expectation

Successful paid activation assigns the order to the selected performer without a separate in-app acceptance step. The performer is expected to perform the Cumro within seven days of that activation. The app records a perform-by date; it does not let the customer book an exact start time.

The performer starts the assignment, completes the required steps and submits evidence for platform review. Submission places the order under review; completion is recorded following platform approval. Customers can review available evidence and report concerns to Support.

If the performer cannot fulfil the assignment or the perform-by date is missed, contact Support. Delays require review and do not automatically trigger cancellation, reassignment or a refund.

The current Cumro workflow

The app’s three active ritual steps are Ixraam (intention; internally recorded as niyah), Dawaaf (tawaf), and Sacyiga (sai), in that order. The performer follows the app’s instructions, records and confirms evidence for each active step, and submits the assignment after all three are complete.

These checkpoints describe the app’s service documentation workflow. App status and platform review are not a guarantee of religious acceptance or spiritual outcome.

Evidence and location information

Evidence videos, audio and related records document the physical service and help customers and Support review its performance. Performers must submit authentic evidence for the assigned beneficiary and must not fabricate, substitute or reuse evidence to misrepresent a service.

GPS and location updates are supporting operational information. Signals can be delayed, inaccurate or unavailable. They do not prove an exact position within Masjid al-Haram or independently establish that a ritual was completed.

Respect the privacy of the beneficiary, performers and other people when recording, saving or sharing evidence. Avoid unnecessary recording of bystanders and do not misuse or distribute another person’s information without appropriate authority.

Problems, cancellation, refunds and reassignment

Customers can report missing evidence, delays, concerns about performance, payment problems or cancellation requests through Support. Include the relevant order details, but never send passwords or full card details.

The app does not provide self-service cancellation of a paid assignment, including after the service has started. Contact Support to request a review. The absence of an in-app cancellation button does not remove any rights you may have under applicable law.

Cancellation, refund and reassignment requests are reviewed manually in light of the payment status, work already performed, available evidence and applicable obligations. A support request does not itself cancel an order, guarantee reassignment or authorize a refund. These terms do not promise a fixed refund amount or processing time.

If the selected performer is no longer eligible when a successful payment is being activated, the current payment flow requests a refund through Stripe instead of activating the assignment. A pending refund is not a completed refund; contact Support if the status remains unclear. Other problems after assignment require support review.

Performer access and responsibilities

Performer accounts are manually approved and provisioned by Dawaaf. Public registration creates a customer account and does not allow self-enrolment as a performer. Performer status indicates platform approval, not a separate professional licence or a guarantee of a particular outcome.

Performers are responsible for being able and appropriately qualified to perform the requested Umrah Badal, complying with applicable access requirements and rules, protecting beneficiary information, and truthfully carrying out and documenting their assigned service.

A performer who cannot fulfil an assignment must contact Support promptly, before starting where possible, so the platform can review manual reassignment. There is no self-service decline or cancellation action in the performer workflow. Problems after starting must also be raised with Support; do not falsely mark steps or evidence as complete.

Account security and acceptable use

Keep your account information accurate and your device and sign-in credentials secure. Do not share access to your account. Contact Support promptly if you suspect unauthorized access or activity.

Do not use Dawaaf for fraud, impersonation, unauthorized payments, harassment or unlawful activity. Do not falsify beneficiary details, performer qualifications, evidence or location records; access another person’s private records without authority; interfere with the service; or bypass its security or payment controls.

Privacy and account deletion

The Dawaaf Privacy Policy explains how account, beneficiary, payment, evidence and location information is handled. It is available through Privacy in Customer Account and Performer Profile, and through the Privacy Policy button on this screen.

Customers can request account deletion through Account → Delete account. Active services, unsettled payments or other unresolved issues may need to be reviewed before deletion can be finalized. A deletion request does not itself cancel or refund a paid Cumro. Retention and cleanup are described in the Privacy Policy; deletion is not an immediate erasure of every service or financial record.

Performers should contact Support about closing their account or handling their data. Copies of evidence saved or shared outside Dawaaf are not removed by account deletion.

Availability and interruptions

If an interruption affects an order or prevents a performer from documenting the service, contact Support. An interruption or missing update alone does not establish whether the physical service was performed.
